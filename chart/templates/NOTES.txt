Maintenance Manager for Envoy® {{ .Chart.AppVersion }} is installed in {{ .Release.Namespace }}.

Open the editor (user: admin, password: the one you set):

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward svc/maintenance-manager 8080:8080
  open http://localhost:8080

Nothing reaches your apps until a MaintenanceWindow is armed. At rest, Envoy
never calls maintenance-manager, and if it is ever down, traffic flows to your
apps as if it was not there (fail-open).

One install per cluster: the CRDs and the ClusterRole are cluster-scoped, so a
second release fails Helm's ownership check. Uninstalling keeps your windows,
pages and services (the CRDs carry helm.sh/resource-policy: keep).
