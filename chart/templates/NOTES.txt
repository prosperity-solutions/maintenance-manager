Maintenance Manager for Envoy® {{ .Chart.AppVersion }} is installed in {{ .Release.Namespace }}.

Create a MaintenanceService (which routes), a MaintenancePage (what users see)
and a MaintenanceWindow (when, and what happens) in {{ .Release.Namespace }}.
Shortly before a window starts, maintenance-manager attaches a SecurityPolicy
to the matching HTTPRoutes; the editor (port 8080) arrives in a later release.
Creating or deleting that policy drains the Envoy listener for 60 s: open
connections (WebSockets too) close gracefully and reconnect.

Nothing reaches your apps until a MaintenanceWindow is armed. At rest, Envoy
never calls maintenance-manager, and if it is ever down, traffic flows to your
apps as if it was not there (fail-open).

One install per cluster: the CRDs and the ClusterRole are cluster-scoped, so a
second release fails Helm's ownership check. Uninstalling keeps your windows,
pages and services (the CRDs carry helm.sh/resource-policy: keep), and its
pre-delete hook removes every SecurityPolicy and ReferenceGrant it created.
If the hook fails, run helm uninstall again; --no-hooks leaves them behind.
